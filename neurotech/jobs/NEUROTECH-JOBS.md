🎯 Role Summaries + How to Stand Out

Paradromics — VP Marketing & Brand

What it actually is:

This is a founder-level narrative job. Matt Angle (CEO) just completed the first human BCI implant (June 2025). The company is about to raise a landmark round. They're not looking for a product marketer — they're looking for someone who can make Paradromics the Neuralink alternative that institutional VCs and the public believe in. The narrative has to work on patients, investors, regulators, and the scientific community simultaneously.

Participation pointer:

Before applying, read Matt Angle's 2024 Nature paper on the neural interface architecture. Come with a one-pager: "Three narratives Paradromics should own that Neuralink can't." That's your interview opener. The VP who walks in knowing the science and the story wins. Follow the company on LinkedIn — Matt Angle posts frequently about BCI milestones. Reference a specific post in your outreach.

MBA edge:

You don't need a neuroscience degree — you need to show you can translate "high-density neural recording at 192 channels" into a sentence a pension fund will invest in. Frame it as category creation, not marketing.

Neuralink — Product Operations

What it actually is:

Non-technical project coordinator role at the world's most watched BCI company. You'll be the connective tissue between the engineering team (building the N1 chip) and the clinical team (running the PRIME study). Think: timeline management, cross-functional sync, risk tracking, vendor coordination. It's a $68K–$114K ops role — not glamorous, but近距离 the science.

Participation pointer:

Study Neuralink's FDA approval journey — the FDA rejected them twice before approving the human trial. Know the specific failure points (N1 chip biocompatibility, animal welfare concerns). Ask during interview: "What was the hardest operational lesson from the FDA rejection and how would you structure the ops process differently?" Shows you did your homework.

MBA edge:

MBA gives you the systems-thinking lens: how does a project this complex not fall apart? Frame your experience with multi-stakeholder coordination, not just project management terminology.

LivaNova — Director, Neuromodulation Project Head

What it actually is:

P&L owner for a portfolio of neuromodulation devices (VNS therapy for epilepsy, depression). At $215K–$250K, this is medtech scale — LivaNova is public, established, and has an existing salesforce. You're not building a category; you're maximizing an existing one. Strategy + execution across clinical, commercial, and regulatory.

Participation pointer:

LivaNova's neuromodulation division has been in steady competition with SetPoint Medical and Synchron. Know the competitive landscape: VNS vs. SCS vs. DBS. Read the last two earnings calls for the neuromodulation segment. Come with a one-slide market view: "Where is the growth vector — refractory depression or chronic pain?"

MBA edge:

This is the most MBA-traditional role on the list. The case method training is your advantage here — show structured thinking about portfolio allocation and market entry vs. market deepening. Use frameworks explicitly.

Science Corporation — Head of Demand Generation

What it actually is:

Science Corp makes the PRIMA subretinal prosthesis — a retinal implant for blind patients. This is a $150K–$220K demand gen role for a product that has an FDA approval and is currently scaling commercial operations. You're not creating a category; you're activating a patient community that's been waiting for this technology.

Participation pointer:

The PRIMA implant has 2-year clinical data showing significant visual acuity improvement. The patient community (retinitis pigmentosa, age-related macular degeneration) is passionate and organized. Know the specific patient advocacy groups — Foundation for Retinal Research, RD Support Group. Your demand gen strategy lives and dies by patient referral networks and ophthalmologist relationships. Read the PRIMA clinical papers (2–3 pages, accessible) and come knowing the activation funnel.

MBA edge:

Rare disease + medical device = ultra-specific demand gen. MBA gives you the frameworks for channel strategy and customer acquisition cost modeling that pure marketers miss. Bring a rough CAC analysis for an ophthalmology referral program.

Ampa — Sales Executive

What it actually is:

Ampa appears to be a neurotech peripheral or therapeutic device company. The $150K–$190K band + territory management + medical device context suggests a quota-carrying role. Not a startup founder sales hire — an established company building a sales motion. Remote is real here.

Participation pointer:

Find Ampa's actual product — search "Ampa neurotech" and "Ampa therapeutics" before applying. The job posting is generic. Know what they actually sell. A candidate who walks in knowing the product landscape in their territory and has a territory build plan in hand is a 10x stronger candidate. Ask in interview: "What's the current channel mix — direct to clinic or distributor model?"

MBA edge:

The MBA gives you strategic account planning skills — not just closing, but territory design and long-term relationship architecture. Frame yourself as a strategic territory builder, not a transaction closer.

Theranica — Director of Operations

What it actually is:

Theranica makes the Nerivio device — a remote electrical neuromodulation wearable for migraine (FDA-cleared, smartphone-controlled). This is a digital health company scaling product delivery globally. "Product delivery lifecycle" = supply chain + partner channel management + reimbursement ops.

Participation pointer:

Theranica just closed a Series C (late 2024). They expanded into Europe. The operational challenge at this stage is typically: scaling from 10K to 100K patients, negotiating with insurers for coverage, managing contract manufacturers. Know the digital health reimbursement landscape — FDA-cleared devices still face insurance coverage gaps. Ask: "Walk me through the payer coverage strategy for Nerivio in the US vs. EU."

MBA edge:

Operations in digital health = supply chain + reimbursement + compliance. MBA case prep on CSV (PepsiCo/NNF) is actually relevant here — you're managing a company where the "social value" narrative (migraine relief) and the commercial model have to align. Show you understand the payer landscape.

Attune Neurosciences — Clinical Research Manager

What it actually is:

Attune is an early-stage BCI company focused on anesthesiology applications (preventing anesthesia awareness during surgery). The clinical research manager runs trial operations — site management, IRB submissions, patient recruitment, CRO oversight. It's a hands-on, compliance-heavy role.

Participation pointer:

Anesthesia awareness during surgery is a $1B+ problem (literature estimate). Attune's solution: a closed-loop EEG-based monitoring system. The trial design question: how do you design a trial for a safety-critical device when "no anesthesia awareness" is the desired outcome? Come knowing the FDA's TAP (Total Product Life Cycle) approach for neurological devices. Ask: "How would you structure the pivotal trial given the safety signal?"

MBA edge:

Clinical trial design from a business lens — risk-adjusted trial architecture, CRO vendor selection, site selection for patient diversity. Your MBA training in structured decision-making under uncertainty is directly applicable.

Attune Neurosciences — Trial Recruitment Specialist

What it actually is:

Patient recruitment for a surgical trial — patients undergoing general anesthesia who might experience awareness. The job is community building, site coordination, and referral network activation. $75K–$95K reflects the seniority level — this is a doer role, not a manager role.

Participation pointer:

Anesthesia awareness patients are a specific, hard-to-reach population — most people don't know they've experienced it until it's identified. Recruitment strategies: pre-op anesthesia education, patient forums (FDA's Patient Representative Program), anesthesiology department partnerships. Come with a 30-60-90 day recruitment plan template and you've already shown you can do the job.

MBA edge:

Even for an MBA, this is a "foot in the door" role at a company with potential. The Director path from Clinical Research Manager at Attune is a real trajectory — if the pivotal trial succeeds and the company raises a Series B, the whole clinical org scales. Apply even if you think you're overqualified — the MBA gets you in the door for the next role.
