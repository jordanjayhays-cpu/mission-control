# Call to follow-up

## Inputs
1. The call. If the Granola connector is available, find the meeting by company or person name and read the transcript. Otherwise ask Jordan to paste notes.
2. The goal of the next step (ask if not obvious: book a call, send a proposal, get an intro?).

## Steps
1. **Summarise** in this exact shape:
   - What they want (their words where possible)
   - Concerns or objections raised
   - What we promised
   - Next step + date
   - UNSURE: anything not clear from the notes
2. **Plan:** one line on the email's angle. Drafts first, questions after: never stop to ask before drafting. Fill unknowns with [UNSURE] and list max 3 questions after the draft.
3. **Draft** the follow-up using Placewell email rules:
   - Greeting on its own line
   - One sentence per paragraph
   - No em dashes
   - Under 100 words
   - One clear ask with a specific time option
   - Sign-off:
     Thank you,
     Jordan
     Director of Business Development
     placewellinternational.net
4. **Critique** as the recipient: top 2 weaknesses, then a revised version.
5. **Close the loop:**
   - If Gmail is connected, offer to save it as a draft. Never send.
   - List anything only Jordan must do as "BOARD: ...".
   - Output a one-line pipeline update: Company | Stage | Next step | Date.

## Never
- Invent names, titles, numbers, or commitments not in the notes. Write UNSURE.
- Send email. Drafts only.
