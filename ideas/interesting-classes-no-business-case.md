# Classes worth taking for their own sake — Manila, Jan–Jun 2027

Researched 2026-09-18. Jordan: *"all these are pretty much for Vaya, I would even take an
interesting class."* He is right — the previous list was media, film, photography and food, which is
Vaya with extra steps. These have **no business case** and that is the point.

## 1. Filipino martial arts — Elefant Kali

**Legazpi Active Park, Makati** · elefantkali.com · **₱500 per session**

Arnis, Escrima, Kali, Silat. Group classes, outdoors, described as suited to beginners or people
restarting. The school states its goal as promoting Filipino culture.

**The coincidence worth noticing:** Jordan's housing brief specifies a furnished studio *"near
Legazpi Active Park, Makati."* The national martial art of the Philippines is taught in that park,
for ₱500 a session. He could walk downstairs to it.

Arnis is the declared national martial art. Learning it in Manila, in a public park, from Filipino
instructors, is not something available anywhere else.

## 2. Boxing — Elorde Boxing Gym

**4/F Prudential Life Building, 843 Arnaiz Avenue, Makati** · 02-7102151 · Open 7am–10pm, daily

| | |
|---|---|
| Annual membership | ₱1,200 — drops sessions to ₱250 from ₱350 |
| Unlimited 30 days | ₱4,000 |
| Disciplines | Boxing, Muay Thai, MMA, sparring |

Founded 1974 by Gabriel "Flash" Elorde, world junior lightweight champion for seven years and the
most significant Filipino boxer before Pacquiao. This is a working boxing stable, not a fitness
studio with bags. Membership is not required to start.

At ₱4,000 for unlimited 30 days, this is the cheapest daily structure on any list so far.

## 3. Freediving — Mabini Freediving Club / APNEA Philippines

Classes in **Metro Manila and Mabini, Batangas** · mabinifreediving.com · Molchanovs and AIDA
certification paths

| | |
|---|---|
| Intro session | ₱2,495 |
| Practice / fun dive | ₱1,895 |
| Where | Anilao, Batangas — 2.5–3 hours from Manila |

**The season lines up exactly.** Anilao is diveable year-round but the water is clearest
**November to May**. Jordan is there January to June.

Calm, protected water, best marine life sitting at 5–15m, which is beginner depth. Anilao is one of
the most biodiverse stretches of coast in the world and it is a weekend away.

## 4. Baybayin — Manila For a Day

**Baybayin Crash Course · three hours**

The pre-colonial syllabic script of the Philippines, taught by José Alain Austria (historian),
Luisito Tayag (restoration artist and calligrapher) and Dustin Ancheta. Participants learn the
history and leave able to write their own name in Baybayin.

The National Museum of the Philippines also runs Baybayin writing workshops periodically, and has a
permanent gallery on ancient and traditional Philippine scripts.

Three hours, and afterwards a writing system most Filipinos cannot read is legible to him.
