# Storytelling / communication courses — who actually teaches it in Manila

Researched 2026-09-17, after Jordan asked whether UP, Ateneo, De La Salle and AIM had been
checked properly. They had not been. This corrects that.

## UP — nothing that fits

Checked three arms:
- **UP ISSI** (Diliman) — the business continuing-ed unit. Its catalogue is management, accounting,
  supervision, marketing, maintenance. **No communication or storytelling course.**
- **Department of Speech Communication and Theatre Arts** (UP Diliman) — this is where the subject
  lives at UP, but as **degree coursework**, not short courses for professionals.
- **UP Open University** — non-degree courses, many free, but **online**.

**Verdict: UP does not offer a short in-person storytelling course.** Clean negative.

## De La Salle — unknown, probably not

**School of Lifelong Learning (SoLL)**, DLSU Taft, Manila. Real continuing-ed arm and a
PRC-accredited CPD provider, running certificate courses, public seminars and consultancy.

But it publishes **no communication course list**. The only offering visible publicly is a CPD
seminar series for accountants. Everything else is industry partnerships and in-house programmes.

Worth one phone call, not more: **(632) 8524-4611 loc 396**.

## AIM — yes, and it is a real programme

**The Eloquent Leader: Inspire Through Clear, Confident Communication**
AIM SEELL · Eugenio Lopez Foundation Building, Joseph R. McMicking Campus,
123 Paseo de Roxas, Makati · SEELL@aim.edu · +632 8892 4011

| | |
|---|---|
| Content | Storytelling, speechwriting, audience engagement, persuasive delivery |
| Format | **Hybrid** — one face-to-face afternoon on campus, four live-online mornings, plus a "CEO Talks" forum |
| Fee | **PHP 25,000 / USD 450** |
| Audience | C-suite, business owners, public servants, corporate communicators, senior managers |
| Edition seen | **September 2025.** No 2026 or 2027 edition published. |

Ends with each participant delivering a talk at the CEO Talks Forum. That is a genuinely
distinctive finish and the strongest argument for it.

**The catch:** only one session out of six is in a room. It is mostly an online programme.

## The thing the universities miss

Specialist firms teach this better than the business schools, because for them it is the whole
business rather than one line in a catalogue. All in Makati:

- **Guthrie-Jensen Consultants** — 4/F ACE Building, 101-103 Rada cor Dela Rosa, **Legaspi Village,
  Makati**. Runs public seminars in Communication. No public schedule or fees; enquiry only.
  corporate@guthriejensen.com · (+632) 8816-1610. **This is the same village Jordan is flat-hunting in.**
- **The Public Speaking Institute** — Victoria de Makati, Dela Rosa Street. Certified Public Speaker,
  a six-day communication programme.
- **Rainmakers** — "Power Talks: Mastering the Art and Science of High-Impact Presentations" —
  storytelling, audience analysis, structuring, delivery.

## Recommendation

**Keep Ateneo.** It is the only option verified as fully in-person, it is half the price of AIM, and
the campus is a short hop from Legazpi Village.

**Email Guthrie-Jensen anyway.** They are on the doorstep, they do this as their core trade, and
their public-seminar schedule is invisible until you ask. One email settles whether the university
course or the specialist is the better room.

**Skip AIM for this subject.** The Eloquent Leader is a good programme, but paying 56% more for
something that is five-sixths online defeats the reason for doing this in Manila at all. AIM stays
interesting for a different reason — the seniority of the room — not for storytelling.
