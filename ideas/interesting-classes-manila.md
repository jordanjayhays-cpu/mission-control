# Interesting classes in Manila, cross-referenced to the projects

Researched 2026-09-18. Jordan: *"I just want interesting classes."* Fair — the earlier lists were
business-school catalogues. His projects are media, music, food and interviews. These are the
classes that actually serve them.

## The headline

**Third World Improv** — thirdworldimprov.com · classes in **Makati**, Ortigas and Quezon City

Six levels, each an **8-week term**:

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 · The Joy of Improv | Introduction, short-form games. Makati, Ortigas, QC |
| 2 · Listening and Collaboration | *"Physical and verbal storytelling"*. Makati, QC |
| 3 · Truth and Character | Truth in improv, drawing on personal experience. QC, Ortigas |
| 4 · Group Dynamics | Group mind, introduces long form. **Makati only** |
| 5 · Long Form | Chicago-style formats, with a performance. Makati, Ortigas, QC |
| 6 · Improv Forever | Short-term classes: scene work, music, dance, sketch, comedy writing |

**Why this beats a business-school storytelling course for Jordan specifically:**

- **8 weeks, weekly, in Makati.** That is the anchor — a standing reason to be in a room with the
  same people for two months in a city where he arrives with no network. Ateneo's course is two days.
- **Level 2 is literally "Listening and Collaboration — physical and verbal storytelling."** The
  same subject he picked, taught by doing rather than by slide.
- **Improv's core skill is listening and building on what the other person just said.** That is
  exactly the skill of interviewing a mayor, and exactly the skill of negotiating with Veevy.
- It is interesting, which was the actual request.

It does not replace the Ateneo course. Ateneo is two days in February; an improv term is eight
weeks. They coexist.

**Unknown:** fees, session times and venue addresses are not published. One email settles it.
