## The lead worth checking first, whichever reading wins

The Philippines has a **statutory continuing professional development regime** — CPD units are
required for licence renewal across PRC-regulated professions (nursing, engineering, accountancy,
teaching among them), under what is commonly cited as RA 10912, the CPD Act of 2016.

**UNSURE — not verified.** The act's number, its current amendments, the unit requirements per
profession, and accreditation rules for CPD providers all need checking against the PRC before any
of it is repeated or built on. Treat the paragraph above as a lead, not a fact.

If it holds, it matters: a legal mandate means non-discretionary demand, and it sits directly
beside Placewell, which already deploys licensed Filipino workers who must keep those licences
current while working abroad.
