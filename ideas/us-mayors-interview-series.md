# US Mayors — interview series

Captured 2026-09-15, from Jordan. Idea stage. Nothing built, nothing committed.

## The idea, as Jordan described it

> I had an interesting project for mayors in the United States.
>
> I wanted to find interesting places in the US and interview their mayors.
>
> What types of policies they pass. The things they do. Why they became mayor.

## What it is

Find interesting places in the US. Interview the mayor of each one.

The angle is the person and the place together, not political coverage — a mayor of a
small or unusual American town is an accessible subject who makes real decisions with
visible consequences, and almost nobody interviews them.

## The three questions he named

1. **What types of policies they pass** — the actual legislative record, at a scale small
   enough to see the effect.
2. **The things they do** — the day-to-day of running a town.
3. **Why they became mayor** — the origin story. Why a person takes a job like this.

## Why it could work

- Mayors of small US towns are reachable. City hall phone numbers are public, most have
  no press gatekeeper, and almost none get interview requests.
- "Interesting places" is a repeatable selection mechanic, so the format has an engine.
- Policy at town scale is legible — one ordinance, one visible outcome. National politics
  is not.
