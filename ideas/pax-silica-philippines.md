## Why this matters to Placewell, not just to the assignment

**A 4,000-acre industrial hub is a hiring event.** Construction, then facilities, then technical and
back-office staffing, then the services layer around it. Placewell is a staffing and recruitment
business. This is the largest single manpower demand signal in the Philippines in years, and the
construction start is reported as early 2028.

**Jordan is in the Philippines January to June 2027 — the planning year.** That is the window when
contractors are selected and manpower requirements are scoped, and it is before the hiring itself
starts. Being early costs a meeting, not capital.

**UNVERIFIED and it decides everything:** whether Placewell's licences and sector experience cover
industrial and construction manpower in-country, or only the outbound placement business. If it is
only outbound, this is interesting context and nothing more. **Check before spending time on it.**

## What it means for New Bali — honestly, it cuts against it

The instinct is to read this as tailwind for a Philippine special-zone play. It is not.

1. **The gravity moved to Luzon.** A US-backed AI-native hub with a 4,000-acre land bank is exactly
   the pull that takes corporate tenants away from a beach island in the Visayas.
2. **AI and semiconductor infrastructure is power- and water-hungry.** Bantayan has neither —
   reportedly all 72 test wells nitrate-contaminated, and a chronic water and sanitation gap. New
   Bali should not chase heavy industry or data centres. It never could.
3. **What survives is the people layer.** Remote workers, services, and the staff-and-relocation
   business. That was already the plan; this confirms it rather than changing it.

**The useful version of the connection:** Luzon gets the fab-adjacent industry. Cebu's realistic
play is the skills and services tier. New Bali's realistic play is where those people want to live.
Three tiers, not three competitors.
