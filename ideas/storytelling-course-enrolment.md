scheduling gymnastics.

It is the one skill that touches every venture, because all of them come down to persuading someone:

- **Philosophical King** — the entire product is narrative
- **Vaya** — media and experiences in a country Jordan is new to
- **US Mayors interview series** — a format that lives or dies on story structure
- **Placewell / Amigo Sales** — proposals, cold outreach, the THN counter-proposal
- **Massage Club** — the studio pitch that currently goes unanswered

## The honest tradeoff

a room with people for three months in a new city. If that matters, pair it with one Saturday series
and treat the storytelling course as the intensive.
